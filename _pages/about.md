I build Python pipelines to model complex data, quantify uncertainties, and test the robustness of scientific results. My primary toolkit includes statistical modelling, Bayesian inference, and Monte Carlo simulation. I'm completing a PhD in Astrophysics at Lancaster University (expected February 2027), and was previously a Research Assistant at NASA Goddard Space Flight Center. 

I'm looking for **quantitative research or data science roles from February 2027**, in London or Brighton, where rigorous statistical modelling using cutting edge techniques drives decisions.
