I am a PhD Astrophysicist researcher who uses Python programming and statistical analysis to solve complex problems, work with large datasets, and extract reliable insights from data. 

My work sits at the intersection of Python programming, statistical analysis, and astrophysics research. I build Python data pipelines to clean and validate data before processing, I apply statistical methods such as modelling with Bayesian inference and quantifying uncertanties using Bootstrapping, and test the robustness of my scientific results and conclusions. I have developed experience in SQL and software engineering as a result of undertaking side projects (see my CV for more information).

I'm looking for roles in **quantitative research, data science, data engineering, and software engineering** where rigorous statistical modelling and programming using cutting edge techniques drives decisions. **I am available to work in London or Brighton starting from February 2027**, which is when I am due to complete my PhD.
